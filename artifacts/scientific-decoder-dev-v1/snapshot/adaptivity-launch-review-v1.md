## One substantive specification issue and prospective resolution

Interpreter fixtures and import success cannot certify representability of hidden laws. I flagged that unresolved prerequisite. Under that choice, failure cannot identify model weakness separately from grammar/length/numerical restrictions, task support or finite-data identifiability. The final protocol now states this resolution explicitly; score thresholds and scheduled calls are unchanged.

## Code and ordering checks

- The completed packet must have exactly four string fields and action `propose`. Strict JSON rejects duplicate keys and nonstandard nonfinite constants; the bounded AST interpreter validates the formula. Empty/invalid expressions and numerical-domain failures consume the original slot, receive primary zero and prevent gate passage. Textual explanations do not select or score candidates. Packet validation after response freeze is acceptable: malformed completed answers are retained outcomes, not a reason for replacement.
- The repaired scorer requires the four ordered integer task entries, canonical training-output paths, and 64 ordered finite successful responses per COMPLETE training output before any confirmation request. It validates all eight pair records, their exact 16-slot identities and provider evidence, and requires the frozen bank to equal the validated pair population. It captures response bytes before target generation. Existing confirmation artifacts block another target attempt. Source/bank/response hashes are rechecked before writing the result. The known training-ledger correction is present in the inspected final scorer.
- The renderer uses only whitelisted public descriptions, variable order/bounds/units and either no observations or the 64 training pairs. It does not access confirmation coordinates, law version/difficulty, seeds or other responses. Byte-duplicate prior prompts remain explicit scheduled calls. The neutral actor directory and response-only transport constrain the intended information flow; they do not constitute a hostile-caller or shared-filesystem sandbox.
- The primary score preserves the all-point failure rule. Secondary utility retains every target position, assigns zero to failed predictions and retains finite positions, with coverage and exact-zero target counts. Both model repetitions, four laws and two domains remain visible; the gate is a finite allocation rule, not a significance test. The global metric can emphasize large responses. A high score need not imply accurate low-magnitude behavior, symbolic equivalence or adaptive-query value.
