AlphaResearch-RL · Synthetic development pilot

A sequential interface, a repeated script.

Inspect the saved greedy behavior of base, SFT and SFT + RLOO actors on six synthetic tasks. SFT and RL followed the same seven-step script on every task and selected initial candidate 0. This pilot shows no incremental RL benefit or adaptive financial research.

Recorded actions, reconstructed context

The original reports saved generated text and effective actions, but no prompt tokens or observations. The state and compact actor-input panels below are reconstructed by CPU replay under the pinned environment. They are not authenticated historical prompts or recovered model reasoning.

The tasks are deliberately synthetic signal, null and decay generators. Rewards are later synthetic rank IC minus research cost, not market returns or financial alpha. This is a development pilot, not an untouched final test.
