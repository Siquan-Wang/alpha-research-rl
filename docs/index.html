What the completed experiments show

  • With matched starting states, truthful feedback did not improve mean candidate Q over masking. Its relative selector gain was positive, but its gain over keeping the prefix winner was negative. All thirteen gate checks are retained.
  • Astra generated six proposals per episode under three feedback conditions. Full feedback's mean future IC was −0.033823, with no observed benefit over the validity-only condition; all 30 assessments were valid.
  • The separate pool diagnosis found missed hindsight opportunity, while the first-proposal and minimum-AST rules still had negative mean IC. A positive oracle ceiling is not a usable strategy.
  • Both original RL runs improved the declared reward over SFT. Most of that gain came from fewer invalid proposals; neither sampled policy exceeded the uniform formula-grid reference.
  • Correctly linked RL also exceeded its matched reward-permutation control in both seeds. The predictive-IC contribution changed in opposite directions across seeds, and greedy behavior was unchanged.
  • Saved training-credit accounting found zero direct validity coefficients in 61 of 63 recorded updates. Evaluation gains from fewer failures and training credit are different quantities; this does not identify gradient shares or financial learning.
  • The sequential evidence-acquisition gate failed. Candidate-bank generation and sequential-controller training for that branch stopped.
