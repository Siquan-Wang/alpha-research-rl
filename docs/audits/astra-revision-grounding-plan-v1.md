# Matched-prefix public-rationale audit: outcome-blinded plan

**Status: plan only, written 2026-10-01 at approximately 11:47 UTC, after
collection began.** No current execution/batch records, partial proposals or
revision-study outcome reports were inspected to draft it. This is an ancillary
descriptive review, not a prospective primary endpoint, new generation experiment
or modification of the frozen study. Root must authorize access to the complete
80 prompt/response pairs before coding begins.

The author also wrote the study protocol; this is not an independent review of
that protocol or external peer review. Earlier v1 and pool results are already
known. “Outcome-blinded” here means no inspection or lookup of current-study
future outcomes while coding; it does not erase prior knowledge of reused
development data or blind the reviewer to the visible feedback condition.

## Fixed material and questions

Review **all 80 hosted final packets**: ten states × truthful/masked × four
repetitions. Exclude the 120 deterministic cheap packets from this text audit.
Use only each supplied prompt, exact public response, fixed interface and
necessary expression syntax. Do not read other responses to judge what an actor
could know. No market calculations, future-cache lookup, new actor call, external
information retrieval, response repair or request for hidden reasoning occurs.

The permitted texts are `hypothesis` and `revision`, with `expression` used to
check their referent. The [frozen protocol](../astra-matched-prefix-plan-v1.md#exact-treatment-and-actor-observation)
and [prompt builder](../../src/alpha_research_rl/astra_revision_core.py) distinguish
common numerical initial probes from the two candidate bundles, which are null
in the masked condition. A concise economic hypothesis need not cite a number.

| Reviewer question | Check and boundary |
|---|---|
| Is a cited observation correct? | Match the named formula/probe, metric, sign, units, historical window and value to its prompt field. Do not substitute another record that happens to match the number. |
| Is unavailable feedback asserted as supplied? | Separate a displayed value, explicit mathematical inference, qualitative conjecture, and a claim of a reported measurement with no supplied basis. A null candidate bundle does not erase common probes. |
| Is the interpretation faithful? | Distinguish raw mean IC, its absolute magnitude, broker orientation, dispersion, support/coverage and individual subwindows. An ordering must follow the cited metric; “stable” without a defined criterion may remain ambiguous. |
| Does the stated revision describe its formula? | Check explicit window/operator/sign statements against the expression. A proposed economic mechanism is an untested hypothesis, not a demonstrated causal explanation. |
| Does confidence exceed the evidence? | Flag explicit claims of demonstrated future gain, guaranteed profitability, statistical significance without a supplied test, or completed evaluations absent from the prompt. Aspirations and qualified hypotheses are not automatic errors. A text claim about a tool action does not prove that action occurred. |

For the known sign-transformed shared probe, an explicitly derived sign change
can be supported: identify the source formula and state the transformation.
IC sign reverses; dispersion and valid support remain unchanged under exact
negation. Do not extend that inference to unrelated windows or nonlinear
transforms. A mathematically consistent but unlabelled derived value is marked
**source attribution ambiguous**, not automatically an invented report.

## Coding, rounding and citations

Review packets in fixed chronological state, condition and repetition order.
Keep one coverage row per planned packet. Preserve malformed or missing packets
as **not text-assessable**, without substring rescue or replacement. If the
complete 80-pair population cannot be verified, report incomplete coverage and
withhold a complete-audit headline.

Split factual assertions into atomic claims. Assign each one:
**displayed-supported**, **derived-supported**, **contradicted**,
**unsupported reported measurement/action**, or **ambiguous**. Separately mark
untested hypotheses and unsupported-certainty language. Absence of a quantitative
claim is **not applicable**, not a factual error or proof of good grounding.
Record secondary issues without counting the same claim twice in the main
classification. Do not produce a composite “reasoning quality” score.

Compare a stated number after explicit unit conversion. For ordinary rounded
decimals, accept the interval within half a unit of its last displayed digit;
apply the equivalent rule to scientific notation, with at most `1e-12` numerical
slack. Counts stated as exact integers must match exactly. Label correctly
rounded values as rounded, not exact. Undefined “roughly,” unspecified units or
unclear referents remain ambiguous when precision cannot be inferred. Do not
invent a tolerance for qualitative words. Check sign and ordering against full
supplied precision; a rounded near-zero value alone cannot establish an exact
tie. Verified algebraic derivations require the equation and its assumptions.

Every coded finding retains the packet identifier, prompt and response SHA-256,
public file reference, JSON field and zero-based Unicode character span in the
decoded text, plus a short exact quote. Cite the corresponding observation JSON
path/value or instruction line and explain the comparison or missing basis.
For an unsupported claim, identify the claimed referent and relevant null or
absent field; do not assert absence from unexamined external evidence. Unresolved
interpretations stay visible. Never publish private paths, thread IDs or stderr.

## Reporting and exposure boundary

Report coverage out of **80**, each condition out of **40**, and all ten states.
For each condition show packets with at least one contradicted/unsupported claim,
ambiguous claim, derived citation and no factual claim. These packet categories
may overlap and need not sum to 40. Separately report claim counts and the number
of applicable citation opportunities; verbosity changes that denominator.
Keep all coded records, including clean and unassessable rows, rather than only
interesting quotations. No significance test or prediction-quality ranking is
planned.

Freeze the coding table and exposure log before requesting any current outcome
report. Record any accidental exposure; do not preserve a false blinding claim.
Textual fidelity cannot establish hidden reasoning, intent, actual tool absence,
causal feedback use or predictive improvement. This audit changes no response,
selector, score, allocation rule, experiment status or next-study authorization.
