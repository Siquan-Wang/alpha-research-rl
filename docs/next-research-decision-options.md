# Next research decision: useful evidence, not another activity count

Written 2026-10-01 **before reading any frozen-pool diagnosis outcomes**.
This is design advice. None of the proposed calls, training budgets, splits or
thresholds below is an adopted protocol. It does not restart a stopped financial
study or the deferred local-model branch.

**Prefer a bounded hosted-Astra proposal-quality intervention if the pool
diagnosis leaves a concrete generation-versus-selection uncertainty. Otherwise
stop further same-interface financial calls for now.** A constructed acquisition
experiment is a separate, narrower learning question worth considering only if
that becomes the priority. A Transformer forecasting branch currently receives
zero new compute: it would expand scope without resolving the demonstrated gaps.

## Why another experiment needs a specific job

The [Astra study](astra-agent-results-v1.md) found a negative full-minus-validity
mean future-IC difference, with every selected assessment valid and only one
trajectory per arm/period. The [local reward controls](reward-linkage-results-v1.md)
showed higher penalized reward under correct linkage, but inconsistent predictive
components and identical greedy formulas. Their [generation MCSEs](reliability-analysis-v1.md)
are material and rare failures matter. More evaluations of those same policies
would narrow a conditional sampling question without supplying adaptive behavior.

The [financial sequential gate](sequential-gate-results-v1.md) failed its
chronological consistency rule. It remains stopped. The separate
[constructed query gate](mechanism-gate-results-v1.md) proved an exact .18
adaptive opportunity; it did not test a language policy. The portfolio already
has functioning orchestration, actual local weight updates and careful replay.
Its next useful evidence should concern improved proposals or learned decisions.

| Alternative | Question it can answer | Main limitation | Proposed ceiling if separately adopted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Matched-prefix Astra revisions | Does historical numerical feedback improve a fresh formula and the result of a fixed selector? | Conditional development evidence; hosted weights and randomness are not fully attested | 80 model calls; 200 attempted proposal slots including cheap controls |
| 2. Learned constructed acquisition | Does correctly linked RL teach appropriate sequential branching beyond its parent and permutation controls? | A small, deliberately solvable synthetic decision algorithm; no financial generation | Parent preflight first; at most four runs ×32 groups ×8 trajectories, plus frozen evaluation, within four GPU hours |
| 3. Market-sequence Transformer | Does sequence representation improve direct prediction beyond cheap numerical baselines? | Different research target, with no current evidence that architecture is the bottleneck | Zero now; a separate forecasting protocol would be required |

## 1. Hosted Astra: revise a formula, not select the bigger displayed number

**State and intervention.** A concrete candidate design uses one common state
per existing half-year: the first two proposals of v1's withheld-feedback
episode, selected by this fixed index rule regardless of future quality. Give
both conditions the same initial probes, formulas, order, grammar status and
DSL. Remove historical hypothesis/revision narratives from both prompts because
they can reveal the masked evidence. Truthful historical candidate metrics are
visible in one condition and masked in the other. This is a new controlled
interface, not a claim to reproduce the original actors' hidden states.

Obtain four fresh continuations per exact prompt, with one new attempted formula
each: ten states ×two evidence conditions ×four repetitions = **80 calls**.
Predeclare interleaved launch order. Repetitions are evaluated separately; their
candidates are never pooled into a larger best-of-four search. No free repair,
duplicate replacement or extra generation is allowed.
Four repetitions are a bounded feasibility choice, not a power calculation or
precision guarantee; a noisy result can remain inconclusive at that fixed budget.

**Controls and budget.** For each state/repetition also produce one candidate
from each of three frozen cheap generators: copy the historical winner; make
one scheduled legal AST/window edit to it; sample from a bounded grammar using
predeclared seeds. That is 120 cheap slots and **200 total attempted slots**.
Freeze mutation order and grammar probabilities before calls, with one proposed
candidate per slot and no uncounted search among alternatives. Shared caching
reduces CPU work, never the denominator. At most 200 new unique feedback and
200 new unique assessment evaluations are needed; prefix outcomes reuse the
completed pool ledger. Actual calls, wall time and tokens are reported separately.

**Endpoint.** Define `Q(f)` as feedback-oriented future IC, or -1 for an invalid
or unscorable factor. Directions are fixed from truthful historical feedback
for every generator, regardless of the actor's evidence condition. Let `b` be
the common prefix's feedback-selected factor. For each new `f`, keep both
`Q(f)` and

`G(f) = Q(select(prefix + f)) - Q(b)`,

where `select` is the frozen historical-only selector with earliest ties.
The primary contrast is mean `G(truth)-G(masked)`; mean candidate-quality
difference is a prespecified secondary that distinguishes generation from
acceptance by the selector. Invalid proposals remain in `Q(f)` and in the
selection process. A copied parent gives zero quality gain. With two prefix
attempts plus one continuation, the common search cost is .03; the incremental
cost-adjusted improvement over the two-attempt prefix is `G-.01`. This is
abstract research cost, not transaction cost or provider billing.

Freeze all 200 slots before new assessments. Average repetitions inside each
state, then give all ten states equal weight. The replication unit for generation
is the fresh continuation conditional on a fixed state; market periods remain
dependent. Four repetitions give coarse generation-variability estimates and
cannot support a general financial effect. Report every state/year and validity
accounting; new proposals and zero observed failures do not create independent
markets or prove zero failure probability.

**Stop/go.** A possible engineering allocation rule is to require positive mean
primary and candidate-quality contrasts versus masking, and truthful-feedback
performance above each frozen cheap generator on both quantities, with gains
not attributable solely to validity penalties. Failure ends this version; no
extra repetitions, prompt search or favorable-state filtering. Passing would
justify considering a separate validation study, not claim significance or
alpha. These are proposed criteria to review before adoption, not a current gate.

## 2. Constructed acquisition: a focused learning result, separate from finance

Reuse only the already registered kernel: four hidden candidates, three binary
tools, exactly two distinct queries, then actual terminal selection. Its adaptive
oracle is .81 and strongest fixed-query policy, also using both responses at
selection, is .63. The primary outcome is exact expected success of the learned
policy; a second endpoint requires coarse-first and the appropriate second query
under **both** possible first responses. Wrong first queries count as failures
in the full presentation-bank denominator.

This option would require a newly adopted policy protocol. There is no completed
local-model preflight to continue. Group the 1,152 raw relabelings into the 144
unique complete actor-visible presentations before splitting; an illustrative
fixed allocation is 96 training, 12 parent-preflight and 36 evaluation. Hash the
whole prompt/token interface and keep all aliases in the same bank. These are
relabeled versions of one algorithm, not unseen financial mechanisms.

Start a fresh zero-effect adapter on the available local Qwen backbone, with
no Bayes-action SFT teacher. Legal-action masking removes format repair from
the reward. Verify the exact sampled/logged distribution and gradients in a
small, bounded unscored check. Parent preflight needs at most 12×7=84 greedy
decision calls to integrate its trees exactly. If parent value is at least .80,
stop: the remaining learning headroom is at most .01.

A candidate training ceiling is two correctly linked runs and two matched
reward-permutation controls, each 32 groups of eight three-decision trajectories:
**1,024 trajectories, at most 128 updates**, no selected intermediate checkpoint.
Compare with the parent, Bayes oracle, strongest fixed schedule and random-query
reference. Evaluate the final five policies on all 36 reserved presentations
with their actual terminal choices: at most 1,260 decision calls. Use an unscored
throughput measurement before registration; if the complete comparisons cannot
fit four local GPU hours, do not adopt this budget. Incomplete execution is not
evidence of a learning failure.

The earlier design's candidate go rule remains useful: both correct seeds exceed
their parent and paired controls, reach at least .72 expected success, and pass
the proper-branch condition on at least 90% of the full evaluation bank. The .72
criterion captures half of the .18 oracle-minus-fixed gap. It is an allocation
rule, not significance. Training seeds are learning replications; exact tree
integration removes tool-noise Monte Carlo error for a fixed policy, not
training uncertainty. Fair-bit replacement yields .25 by construction and is
only a boundary check. No market data enters; no Astra weights are trained;
successful learning would still be learning a small conditional algorithm.

## 3. Transformer forecasting: decline the scope expansion now

A defensible future question would compare sequence information with lagged
features using an identical target, universe, preprocessing and chronology.
Its endpoint should include a declared forecast loss and IC against last-return,
ridge and a small nonlinear baseline; the unit is a forward market block, with
training seeds nested within it. It would need explicit purging, train-only
normalization, model-selection boundaries and a fixed architecture budget.

None of those missing forecasting comparisons explains whether Astra uses
feedback to improve proposals. A larger architecture cannot supply new data
vintages, pretraining isolation or independent periods. The current .01759 ridge
IC and interval crossing zero are not evidence that attention is the missing
ingredient. Allocate **zero new fits or GPU hours** here unless direct market
representation learning becomes a separate project objective. No Transformer
gate or budget is adopted by this note.

## Decision after the pool diagnosis

If the full-feedback oracle's mean oriented IC is nonpositive, another selector
cannot rescue those exact pools. Deprioritize another same-interface financial
batch; this is an allocation judgment, not a bound on a new generator. If the
oracle is positive, inspect the already frozen simple selectors and the signed
task/year results. Headroom alone does not imply a learnable selector. If a cheap
rule already explains the available gain, report that fact before considering
more sophisticated selection.

Option 1 is preferred only when a real residual question about *new proposal
quality under controlled feedback* remains and the full bounded comparison is
worth its cost. Option 2 is a possible later change of scientific priority, never
an automatic fallback that restarts a deferred branch. Choosing no new model
experiment is valid. The stopped financial gate and curriculum remain stopped;
2025+ remains sealed. Reused development periods, revised historical data and
unaudited foundation-model exposure constrain every financial interpretation.
The [primary-source design context](agentic-research-design-context.md) supplies
prior-work boundaries; neither this decision table nor a positive follow-up
would establish methodological novelty by itself.

## POST-RESULT decision — 2026-10-01

This section was appended **after** reading the completed
[pool diagnosis](../results/astra_pool_diagnosis_v1.json). The pre-result advice
above is preserved. This is a recommendation for a separately reviewed protocol,
not adoption or execution of another experiment.

**Recommend one revised, bounded 80-call hosted-Astra intervention.** Its purpose
is to identify whether numerical feedback improves a new proposal from an
identical research state. Decline another unmatched six-turn batch, selector
tuning on these outcomes, and any local-model or Transformer restart.

### What the pool diagnosis changes

All 180 slots were valid; the diagnosis used 108 new CPU evaluations and 24
reused keys, with zero model calls. The relevant fixed-panel IC summaries are:

| Arm | All six candidates, equally weighted | Original selection | Hindsight oracle |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Full feedback | −.021811 | −.033823 | +.015711 |
| Validity only | −.011689 | −.027459 | +.024480 |
| Withheld feedback | −.009603 | −.028719 | +.029464 |

Full feedback has a lower realized oracle ceiling than both controls and a
**smaller** mean selection gap: .049534 versus .051939 and .058183. Thus its
observed deficit cannot be described solely as unusually bad final selection.
These are descriptions of realized pools, not identified causal contributions
of generation or evidence. In particular, the full-minus-validity first-proposal
contrast is +.008553 even though that proposal precedes incremental feedback.
Different initial draws already contribute variation.

All three first-proposal and minimum-AST selectors still have negative mean IC.
Even every arm's hindsight oracle has negative utility after the fixed .06 cost.
The full-feedback oracle is also negative in 2021 (−.041236 IC). Positive average
oracle IC is neither a learnability result nor a reason to run automatically.
The residual question is whether the unfavorable candidate-quality pattern
persists under **matched states and repeated generation**, rather than whether
an oracle can select a favorable expression after seeing its future.

### Revisions required before adoption

1. **Make candidate quality primary.** Keep the ten fixed states specified
   above: the first two withheld-arm proposals per half-year, including every
   unfavorable year. Use all four continuations per condition separately. Let
   `Q(f)` be feedback-oriented future IC, with −1 for an invalid or unscorable
   proposal. The primary contrast is
   `mean_t[mean_r Q(truth_t,r) − mean_r Q(masked_t,r)]`, with exactly ten equally
   weighted states and four attempts per state/condition. This explicitly
   revises the pre-result endpoint ordering: `G` becomes the secondary measure
   of how proposal quality passes through the unchanged historical selector.
   All three cheap-generator comparisons use the same state averaging.

2. **Correct the copy interpretation.** In the earlier phrase “zero quality
   gain,” zero refers to `G`, not `Q`. For the historical winner `b`,
   `Q(copy)=Q(b)`, which need not be zero; `G(copy)=0`, and its incremental
   cost-adjusted gain is −.01. Initial probes provide context but are not extra
   selectable candidates unless already present among the two prefix proposals.
   A duplicate from *any* generator consumes its attempt, reuses its factor's
   `Q`, and adds no candidate to the selector. It is not assigned −1 merely for
   duplication. Report duplicate rates separately; four repeated copy slots
   are a reference with multiplicity, not four independent generations.

3. **Give every generator one attempt.** The mutation control makes one
   scheduled edit; the grammar control makes one draw from a distribution
   frozen before collection. Fix edit order, grammar probabilities, seeds and
   expression limits prospectively. Do not redraw malformed, duplicate,
   over-limit or unusable expressions. Keep all 40 slots per cheap generator
   and all 80 hosted slots. Grammar-invalid or historically unusable attempts
   have `Q=−1` and cannot enter the historical selector, so their `G=0` relative
   to the usable prefix; a historically usable candidate that becomes
   future-unscorable has `Q=−1` and can reduce `G` if selected. No future outcome
   may retroactively change admission. Infrastructure failure makes the study
   incomplete, not a replaceable bad draw or an omitted observation.

4. **Freeze the whole 200-slot bank before assessment.** Freeze the common
   states, two prompt versions, cheap-generator algorithms and source identities
   before collection. Then publish all 80 hosted and 120 cheap attempts, exact
   directions from truthful historical feedback, and every historical selector
   decision before any new future evaluation or result assembly. Reuse matching
   old outcomes only after this freeze; already observed development outcomes
   cannot be made unseen. No favorable-prefix replacement, pooling four draws,
   future sign choice, or best-of-many mutation search is permitted.

5. **Keep the uncertainty claim conditional.** Fresh hosted calls are the
   generation repetitions; the ten states and five years are dependent market
   observations. Matched repetition indices do not imply common random seeds.
   Show every state, year, attempt, validity rate, predictive contribution and
   selector-acceptance rate. Any Monte Carlo error estimate must state its
   unverified independence assumption and the fragility of four draws. Neither
   80 calls nor 200 slots is a count of independent markets. Masking tests the
   availability of historical numerical evidence, not Astra weight learning or
   the truthfulness of its brief decision rationale.

### Prospective allocation decision

If separately adopted, end this version after the fixed bank; do not add calls
to obtain a preferred sign. A candidate rule for considering further financial
work is that truthful feedback beats masking and **each** cheap generator on
mean `Q`, with positive differences in the all-attempt predictive contributions
as well as the invalidity-penalized scores. Also require its mean `G` to exceed
each comparator and its own .01 incremental cost. These are conservative
engineering allocation conditions, not significance thresholds or a power
guarantee. Failure or unresolved sampling noise supports no expansion of this
interface; success would support a separately designed validation question,
not profitability or a new holdout claim.

This experiment is useful because it can distinguish candidate improvement
from selector acceptance using actual proposed formulas, cheap alternatives
and repeated identical states. It still tests one-step conditional research,
not autonomous long-horizon discovery, original factor mechanisms or hosted
weight RL. The existing weekly allowance can support the bounded hosted calls;
unused allowance is not itself a reason to spend it. The stopped financial gate,
deferred local benchmark and untouched 2025+ study boundary remain unchanged.
